Visitors and Green Fees

Visitors

Visitors are always welcome at Holyhead. Pick a date below to see your green fee, then book your tee time online.

Green fees include third-party insurance. Card payments are taken on arrival, and the pro shop has buggies, trolleys and hire clubs. You can also book by calling the office on 01407 763279, option 1.

Hire and extras
Hire clubs £20, buggies £35, electric trolleys £15 and pull trolleys £5, from the pro shop on 01407 762022.
Complimentary golf
On valid proof, we may offer complimentary green fees to serving Captains, Secretaries, PGA Professionals and Head Greenkeepers (BIGGA members), usually up to two per party.
Dress code
Golf shirts with a collar, roll neck or turtle neck; tailored shorts, mid to knee length, with sports socks. Not permitted: denim, beach or football shorts, tracksuits, T-shirts, vests, football or rugby shirts, or shoes without socks. Staying in your golf gear for a drink after your round is usually fine.
Over from Ireland?
Ferries from Dublin arrive at Holyhead, a short drive from the first tee. Stay in our apartments, play a few rounds and eat in the Par 5 Lounge. Stay and play
