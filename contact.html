How to find us

By road. The A55 runs all the way to Holyhead. Follow the B4545 towards Trearddur Bay; the club is on Lon Garreg Fawr, LL65 2YL.

By train. Holyhead station is at the end of the line, with trains from London Euston, Crewe and Cardiff.

By ferry. Ferries from Dublin arrive at Holyhead port, a short drive away.

By air. Liverpool and Manchester airports are around two hours away by car.
