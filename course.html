Course overview

James Braid laid out Holyhead in 1912. It opened with 14 holes on 22 June that year, and the full eighteen followed two years later.

The course opens with a drivable par 4 and finishes with five par 4s in a row, three of them over 400 yards. In between come five par 3s, back-to-back par 5s at the turn and the hardest hole on the course, the 10th, with Holyhead Mountain behind. Fairways are tight and lined with gorse and heather, a few tee shots are blind, and a couple of holes still cross each other as Braid laid them out.

Par 71, 5,999 yards from the whites. Practice ground and a short-game area sit next to the clubhouse.
