Join Holyhead Golf Club

A friendly members’ club on one of the best courses in North Wales, with juniors welcome from the age of six.

  • A James Braid course ranked fourth in North Wales
  • Reduced green fees at other James Braid courses
  • Competitions, roll-ups, the Coffee Club and the Seniors
  • Ladies' roll-ups on Fridays and Sundays
  • Coaching all year with PGA professionals, and a junior roll-up every Sunday at 11am
