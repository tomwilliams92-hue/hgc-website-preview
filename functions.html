Functions

Holyhead Golf Club looks out across the course towards Holyhead Mountain: a lovely setting for weddings, birthdays, christenings and gatherings of every kind.

The clubhouse seats up to 100 people and holds up to 200 for parties. Our caterers will build a menu around your guests’ dietary needs and your budget. Room hire is £50 for members and £100 for non-members, and there is a full-size snooker table and a pool table too.

We cater for

  • Weddings and engagements
  • Birthdays and anniversaries
  • Christenings and baby showers
  • Funerals and wakes
  • Business, club and society dinners
