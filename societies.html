How many golfers make a society?
Twelve or more. For smaller groups, book visitor tee times online.
Can we hire buggies and clubs?
Yes. Buggies £35, trolleys £5 and hire clubs £20, booked with your society.
Where do we eat?
In the Par 5 Lounge in the clubhouse, run by our caterers. Packages B to E include food; tell us about dietary needs on the form.
Can we have a room for prizes?
Areas of the clubhouse can be set aside for golf parties. Mention it in your request.
